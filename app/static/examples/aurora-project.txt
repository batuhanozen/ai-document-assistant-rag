PROJECT AURORA · DELIVERY BRIEF
Synthetic demonstration document. This project, its dates, and its people are fictional.

Purpose
Project Aurora is a document search pilot for the Atlas Labs support team. The pilot covers only synthetic support articles and internal demonstration handbooks.

Timeline
The Aurora pilot starts on 2 November 2026 and runs for six weeks. The internal demo is scheduled for 11 December 2026. Mira Chen is the fictional project owner.

Success criteria
The target is at least 85 percent correct source attribution on a reviewed set of 40 questions. A question with no supporting document must receive an insufficient-information response. Median response time should remain below eight seconds on the demonstration laptop.

Data handling
The pilot must not use customer documents, credentials, or personal data. Uploaded documents are stored only on the local demonstration machine. Deleting a document must also remove its vector records.
