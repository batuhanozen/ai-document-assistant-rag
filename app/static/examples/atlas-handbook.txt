ATLAS LABS · EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Synthetic demonstration document. Atlas Labs and all policies below are fictional.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Submit leave requests in the People Portal at least 10 business days before the first day of leave. Managers respond within three business days.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Wednesday is the shared collaboration day at the Berlin office. Remote work outside Germany requires written approval from the People team.

Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of EUR 1,200. It can cover approved courses, books, and technical conferences. Unused funds do not roll over to the next year.

Equipment
Atlas Labs provides a laptop and a EUR 350 one-time home-office equipment allowance. Contact the IT help desk to request replacement equipment.
